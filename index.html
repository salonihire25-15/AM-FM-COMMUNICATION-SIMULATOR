QUICK THEORY

Amplitude Modulation

The carrier amplitude changes with the message while carrier frequency stays constant.

s(t) = A₍c₎[1 + μm(t)] cos(2πf₍c₎t)

Frequency Modulation

The instantaneous carrier frequency changes with the message; ideal carrier amplitude stays constant.

s(t) = A₍c₎ cos(2πf₍c₎t + β sin(2πf₍m₎t))

FFT and bandwidth

The FFT shows signal frequency components. Single-tone AM has sidebands at f₍c₎ ± f₍m₎ and bandwidth 2f₍m₎.

Noise and SNR

Noise is added in the channel. SNR compares modulated-signal power to added-noise power; higher SNR means less relative noise.
